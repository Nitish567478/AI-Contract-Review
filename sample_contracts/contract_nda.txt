MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of January 1, 2025 ("Effective Date"), by and between Acme Corporation, a Delaware corporation ("Disclosing Party"), and Beta Innovations LLC, a California limited liability company ("Receiving Party"). The Disclosing Party and Receiving Party may collectively be referred to as the "Parties."

1. Term of Agreement.
This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall remain in effect for a period of two (2) years, expiring on December 31, 2026, unless terminated earlier in accordance with Section 3.

2. Confidential Information and Non-Disclosure Obligations.
The Receiving Party agrees to maintain in confidence and protect all Confidential Information disclosed by Disclosing Party with the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of like nature, but not less than reasonable care. Receiving Party shall not disclose, publish, or disseminate Confidential Information to any third party without prior written consent. The obligations of non-disclosure shall survive termination of this Agreement for five (5) years.

3. Termination.
Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time, with or without cause, upon thirty (30) days prior written notice to the other party. Upon termination, Receiving Party shall promptly return or certify the destruction of all Confidential Information.

4. Limitation of Liability.
To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, neither party shall be liable for any indirect, incidental, or consequential damages. Each party's maximum aggregate liability arising out of or related to this Agreement shall be capped at and limited to fifty thousand dollars ($50,000).

5. Governing Law and Jurisdiction.
This Agreement shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflicts of law principles. Any legal suit, action, or proceeding shall be instituted exclusively in the federal or state courts located in New Castle County, Delaware.
